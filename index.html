On-Page SEO Optimization for E-commerce Website

Duration: March 2025 – April 2025
Role: SEO & Paid Media Intern

🎯 Objective

To enhance the website’s organic visibility and increase qualified traffic by implementing on-page SEO strategies tailored to e-commerce best practices.

🔍 Key Responsibilities

  • Website Audit: Performed audits using Screaming Frog, SEMrush, and Google Search Console.
  • Keyword Research: Identified and mapped high-volume keywords using Ahrefs & Google Keyword Planner.
  • Content Optimization: Optimized meta tags, headers, and product descriptions.
  • Image SEO: Added alt tags and compressed images with keyword-rich filenames.
  • Internal Linking: Built strategic links between blog, category, and product pages.
  • Schema Optimization: Integrated JSON-LD product schema for rich snippets.
  • Performance Monitoring: Used Google Analytics and GSC to monitor improvements and KPIs.

📈 Outcome

  • Improved organic traffic by 38% within 6 weeks.
  • Boosted top-10 keyword rankings by 22%.
  • Reduced page load time by 1.5 seconds.
  • Enhanced SERP appearance with rich snippets.
